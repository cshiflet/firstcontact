--- BUNDLED DICT SEED v1 ---
This file is fed to ZDICT_finalizeDictionary as the dictContent
buffer. It becomes the raw content tail of the trained dictionary.
zstd packs the most-profitable bytes at the end, so high-frequency
patterns are placed lower in this file. Edits here change the bundled
dict; bump the bundled-bodies-vN version when content moves.

Section markers below use a `## ` prefix. The trainer strips those
lines before passing the seed to ZDICT_finalizeDictionary (so the
markers don't occupy dict slots), and seed_coverage.py uses them to
group patterns for the coverage report.
